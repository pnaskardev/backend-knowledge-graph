# How to Drill — Interview Mode

[[00 - Home|← Home]]

> Goal: breadth + recall, fast. Fill notes in your own words, then self-test.

## The loop (≈5 min per note)
1. Open a note. Write the **One-liner** from your own understanding (look it up if blank).
2. Fill **Why/when** and **Key trade-off** — interviews probe trade-offs, not definitions.
3. Write one **Recall cue** Q&A. This is your flashcard.
4. Glance at **🔗 Connections** → that's the next note to open.

## Daily rhythm
- **New notes:** fill 15–25 per day following the links (depth-first within a domain).
- **Recall pass:** search the `#review` tag, open 10 random notes, hide the answer, say it out loud.
- Move a note's tag from `#review` to `#solid` once you can answer its cue cold.
