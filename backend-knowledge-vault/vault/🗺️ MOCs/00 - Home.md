## 🧭 Suggested Learning Path
Follow this order — each builds on the last.
1. [[Distributed Systems MOC]] — the mental models everything rests on
2. [[Databases MOC]] — how data is stored, scaled, kept consistent
3. [[Caching MOC]] — make reads fast
4. [[Messaging MOC]] — decouple with queues & streams
5. [[Microservices MOC]] — assemble services from the above
6. [[Kubernetes MOC]] — deploy & operate them
7. [[System Design MOC]] — combine everything into real systems
8. [[Low Level Design MOC]] — OO design (parallel track, anytime)
